**🧪 EVALUATION**

**1. 🔴 EVALUATION OF TECHNICAL PART**
**Extraction Quality**
• Are key fields correctly extracted?
• Are numerical values accurate?

**Source Grounding**
• Are values linked to source evidence?
• Do snippets support the claims?

**Output Structure**
• Is the table clear and consistent?
• Is comparison across studies easy?

**Practical Usability**
• Does the system reduce manual effort?
• Is it easy to review?

**2. 🟣 PRESENTATION**
• Is the problem and solution clearly explained?
• Is the demo showing a clear end-to-end flow from query to structured output?
• Is the demo easy to follow and convincing?
• Does the team demonstrate understanding of limitations and trade-offs?
• Is the system positioned in a realistic clinical/research context?
